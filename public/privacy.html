Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 28, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to StyleVision AI ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information is handled securely and transparently. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile application and related services.

2. Information We Collect

To provide you with our hairstyle, beard, and outfit visualization features, we collect specific types of data:

  • Contact Information (Email Address): When you register or authenticate via Firebase, we collect your email address to secure your account.
  • User Content (Photos): When you upload a selfie or image to simulate styles, the image is transmitted to our secure server for processing. We do not use your photos for any purpose other than providing this styling visualization.
  • Identifiers (User ID & Device ID): We use a unique Account User ID (Firebase UID) to associate subscription status and style credits with your account. Our third-party advertising partner (Google AdMob) may access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to serve personalized advertisements.
  • Purchases (Purchase History): We track your subscription purchases and credit top-up history to deliver active plan features.
  • Diagnostics (Crash Data): We collect anonymous crash logs (via Firebase Crashlytics) to debug errors and improve app performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following specific purposes:

  • App Functionality: Authenticating users, managing credit balances, processing subscriptions, and running AI style simulations on uploaded photos.
  • Third-Party Advertising: Displaying advertisements via Google AdMob using device-level identifiers.
  • Diagnostics & Maintenance: Resolving software crashes and improving server stability.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only share information with third-party service providers (like Firebase and Google AdMob) to host app infrastructure and serve ads, in strict compliance with their terms of service and applicable data protection regulations.

5. Your Choices & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • Ad Tracking: You can opt-out of personalized advertisement tracking in your iOS settings by going to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Account and Data Deletion: You may request complete deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing us at support@stylevisionai.me.
